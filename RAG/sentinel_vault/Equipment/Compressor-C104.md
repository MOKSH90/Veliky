# Compressor C-104 — Equipment Master Record

## Overview
Wet Gas Compressor C-104 recovers overhead hydrocarbons from the Unit 2 separator. It operates downstream of [[Pump-P204]] bottoms heat exchange circuit.

## Operational Dependencies
- Coupled to the Unit 2 vapor circuit. If [[Pump-P204]] suffers unbalance or shutdown, the reduced heat exchange causes liquid carryover risk to C-104 suction knock-out drum.
- Baseline vibration: 1.8 mm/s RMS.
