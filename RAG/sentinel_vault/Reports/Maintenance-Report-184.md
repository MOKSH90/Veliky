# Maintenance Report #184 — Pump P-204 Preventive Maintenance

## Work Completed
1. Executed quarterly lubrication service on [[Pump-P204]] per [[SOP-Pump-Maintenance]].
2. Purged and replenished bearing housings with 45 grams of Mobil Polyrex EM synthetic grease on DE (bearing 6314) and NDE (bearing 6312).
